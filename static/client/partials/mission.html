Mission

Welcome back!

We are aiming for a carefully curated Americana grassroots peer-to-peer digital library to establish freedom of speech and data redundancy. propagate.info indexes infoHashes of public domain educational media, including and limited to Ebooks, Audiobooks, Classical Music, Documentaries, and Renaissance Art.

While inspired by Gazelle's information architecture, this is not a piracy website. We rely on public domain uploads curated by hand by our users. One of our goals is to create a sort of "Renaissance Man."

Anyone anywhere in the world seeking Knowledge may freely download any torrent in the Browser without installing any Software by clicking [WebTorrent] under a Source.

If you want to help build, either upload public domain media (if you can tag carefully and WebTorrent seed) or enter any swarm using a WebTorrent-enabled client.

Webtorrent-enabled Desktop Clients include: BiglyBT w/ WebTorrent Plugin; WebTorrent Desktop; qBitTorrent5.3beta1.
