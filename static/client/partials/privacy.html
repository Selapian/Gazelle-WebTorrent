Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This platform is dedicated to providing public access to public domain works with a commitment to radical transparency and user privacy. Our philosophy is simple: Offer the people free information, without collecting your data.

2. No Accounts, No Cookies

We do not use accounts, profiles, or registration systems. Your identity remains entirely anonymous to us.

  • No Personal Information: We never ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information.
  • No Cookies: We do not place cookies or tracking pixels on your device. Your browsing history on this site remains private.

3. "Snatches" Statistics

To maintain our community Top 10 rankings (calculated by Day, Week, Month, and Year), we record "Snatches"!

  • How it works: When a magnetURI is accessed, we increment a "snatched" counter for that specific work.
  • Historical Data: Snatches are recorded regardless of the work's user upload date. This is subject to change if and when we get grassroots uploads.
  • Anonymity: These counts are purely numerical and aggregate. We do not link snatches to IP addresses or specific user identities.

4. BitTorrent Infrastructure

WebTorrent: Once you join a WebTorrent swarm in the Browser (you do not connect to WebTorrent automatically; you only connect once you download or upload a file), your IP address will be visible to other peers in the swarm. This is a fundamental technological requirement of the BitTorrent protocol.

Desktop Client: Similarly, once you leave this site to join a peer-to-peer swarm on a Desktop Client, your IP address is visible to other peers.

5. Server Logs

Infrastructure Logs: We do not maintain custom server logs. Our infrastructure runs on Amazon EC2, which maintains standard infrastructure logs required for basic network security. We do not analyze these logs for tracking or marketing purposes.

Programming Logs: There may be console.log(err) lines in the server code, which we reserve the right to analyze for basic debugging requirements.

6. Future Roadmap

We are constantly evolving. Please note that if we integrate MetaMask, this privacy policy will be updated to reflect how those technologies interact with your browser and the blockchain. Check back for updates in case New Features are rolled out.
